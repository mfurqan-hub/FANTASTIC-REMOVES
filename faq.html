FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking your move. Can't find your answer? Just give us a call.

Booking & Pricing

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially during peak periods like summer and end of month. However, we do accept last-minute bookings subject to availability — give us a call and we'll do our best.
Pricing depends on the volume of items, distance, time required, and any extra services like packing. We provide a detailed, itemised quote with no hidden fees. What we quote is what you pay.
We may add a small surcharge for properties with many flights of stairs or very difficult access (e.g., no parking nearby). We'll always discuss this with you upfront when you book.
Yes. We ask for 48 hours notice for cancellations or changes. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a small fee. Please call us as soon as possible if your plans change.

Insurance & Safety

Yes. All moves are covered by our comprehensive goods-in-transit insurance up to £50,000. For higher-value items, we offer additional coverage options. Please ask us for details when booking.
In the unlikely event that something is damaged, our insurance will cover the cost of repair or replacement. We ask that you report any damage within 24 hours of delivery so we can process your claim quickly.
Absolutely. All of our team members are fully vetted with DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) checks and receive ongoing training in safe handling and customer service.

On Moving Day

We'll give you a 1-hour arrival window the day before your move. Our team will also call you 30 minutes before arrival on the day. We take punctuality very seriously.
Yes, we supply all packing materials including double-walled boxes, bubble wrap, wardrobe boxes, and packing tape. We can also handle all the packing for you as part of our full packing service.
Absolutely. Our specialist team is trained and equipped to handle pianos, antiques, artwork, safes, and other unusual items. Please mention any special items when requesting your quote so we can plan accordingly.
We understand that move days don't always go to plan. We can hold your items in our vehicles for a short period or arrange short-notice storage if needed. We'll always work with you to find a solution.

Coverage & Storage

Yes, we operate across the whole of the UK including London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Bristol, Edinburgh, and everywhere in between. International moves can also be arranged — please contact us for a bespoke quote.
Yes. We offer secure, climate-controlled storage facilities for both short and long-term use. Our storage units are CCTV monitored 24/7 and are available in a range of sizes to suit your needs.
There's no minimum or maximum storage period. Whether you need storage for a few days between moves or several months, we can accommodate you. Pricing is flexible and based on the space you use.
